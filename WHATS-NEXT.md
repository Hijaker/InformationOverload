# What's Next for Information Overload

## Havoc Board

The Havoc Board will bring the assignments of party members using Information Overload into one view. See each assignment's map, rank and modifiers, alongside your own stats for that map, just like the current Mission Board.

Any party member using the board will be able to press **Play** on any shared assignment. This asks the assignment owner to start the game's normal mission vote, subject to the owner's launch permissions and the game's eligibility checks.

The ability to place your vote for which of the assignements available to you, that you'd like to play.

Preset your readiness state, to automatically accept a "Mission Start" popup-

- **Ready** automatically accepts any map you've voted for that you'd like to play.
- **No choice yet** leaves the normal game popup for you to answer.
- **Not Ready** automatically declines.

If a mission cannot start, the board aims to show who is blocking the start and why, where the game makes that information available. For example that they're loading in, or haven't taken out their weekly Havoc Rewards cache.

## Talents and blessings

**In the Psykhanium**, the plan is to add a Hyper-Violence breakdown and show more detail for individual talents and blessings. You'll be able to choose which recorded blessing and perk setups to compare in Psykhanium Stats. Damage per second against a single armour type is also planned for more focused comparisons.

**In live missions**, the first step is to count relevant events. For example, a breakdown could show that Headtaker was observed at five stacks on 800 out of 1,000 eligible hits. That tells you how often you brought the bonus into play. For now this is a WIP, the first goal was to try to get detailed breakdowns with numbers for talents, but for now we haven't found a reliable way to accomplish this, so first step will be pinning down Event-Based Attribution.

Other areas to explore include reload time saved by using reload speed talents, debuff stacks, such as brittleness and Skullcrusher, how many enemies you were able to stagger due to an Impact Talent or Blessing, and how often a cleave bonus lets an attack reach additional targets, and how many that might be.

## Weapon Breakdowns

A goal is to allow you to sort the weapons by class. The Tactical Axe or the Combat Blade are available to a lot of different classes. But a Zealot and a Psyker should not expect similar outputs from the Combat Blade, by virtue of the difference between their available modifiers to Melee Attacks, Crit Chance, Backstab and more.

## Peril, Stamina and engagement distance

Peril is an important stat for Psykers, and being able to see your own Peril status across a game, and compare to other Psykers to see if you're spending too much time on low Peril is a good starting point to improving as a Psyker.

Stamina is an oft-forgotten stat, and for a lot of people it's simply "The Sprint Bar", getting more detailed stats on Stamina Usage, in relation to both sprinting, blocking and pushing can help you pin down areas that you might be able to improve.

Several Talents and Blessings care about your distance to your targets, with a detailed breakdown of the distance to enemies you deal damage to, will help you evaluate if a blessing like Deathspitter is really worth it for how you play, or it just looks like it should be.

## Overflow Meter

The planned integration with LucLeto's **Overflow Meter** will let you save its stats in Information Overload and revisit them with your mission results. This also opens up more detailed breakdowns for Toughness recovery and related talents and blessings.

## Hit tracking and overkill

Improve hit tracking and the useful damage and overkill breakdown for explosives, the Inferno Force Staff and the Flamer. For explosives, a longer-term goal is to link delayed blasts to the shot that fired them. For flame weapons, the aim is to distinguish direct hits from damage over time more reliably.

Other combat details to explore include stagger, suppression, impact, grab assists (Giving a trapper an "Assist" in your down or death, if you're downed while trapped) and more concrete contributions from companions such as the Cyber-Mastiff and Servo-Skull.

## Major boss encounters

Starting with the Prophet of Decay, the plan is to give major fights their own breakdown. See how long the encounter took, how much each player dealt to the boss and to other enemies, and the kills, downs and deaths during the fight. Complete encounters could contribute to fastest fight records and filtered averages.

## Comparing Missions

In a future patch, we're planning on adding the ability to compare 2 matches to each other. Maybe you played with the same group, on the same map, with 2 different builds, and want to see what those 2 builds brought to the table, maybe you realise that one of the builds has a problem handling specific situations that you wouldn't have realised just by seeing the games by themselves.

## Expansion to the Scoreboard

What started out as a "Keep track of things" mod, has expanded to what can only be called an unnecessary amount of detail. We realised that we have basically created a scoreboard mod, so our goal is to look into how much more we could/want to add on the in-mission scoreboard.
