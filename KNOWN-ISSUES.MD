# Known Issues

This page tracks known issues in InformationOverload and the conditions under which they occur.

## Delayed Explosion Weapons

### [Rumbler and Grenadier Gauntlet hits can be left out of their per-hit figures]

Rumbler and Grenadier Gauntlet shoot grenades, and their damage lands after the shot, not as it fires.
Each grenade is matched back to the shot that fired it.
When a hit can't be matched to its shot for certain, such as with several grenades in the air at once, that shot is left out of the weapon's per-hit figures rather than credited to the wrong one.
Damage and kills still count in full, so only the per-hit figures for these two weapons can come out a little low.
