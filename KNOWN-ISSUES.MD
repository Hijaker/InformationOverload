# Known Issues

This page tracks known issues in InformationOverload and the conditions under which they occur.

## Psyker Force Staves

### [Psyker Force Staves are currently entirely untracked]

Currently any type of data for Force Staves is missing.
It's not a bug, they were just more complicated than hitscan ranged weapons, and Melee took a bit of effort.
A goal was to get specific damage breakdowns, adjusting for talents and similar, to help you calculate the value of talents and blessings, so chronology of attacks was a high priority. This has since changed, so this should be relatively easy to fix, as chronology isn't as important with the new solution in the works.

It's in the works, hopefully will be in a release in a few days at a maximum. [Tuesday - 2026/11/10]

## Delayed Explosion Weapons

### [Rumbler and Grenadier Gauntlet are currently not tracking hits and proper damage breakdowns]

Rumbler and Grenadier Gauntlet shoot grenades, and their damage is not immediate on the attack firing.
Grenadier Gauntlet should be relatively simple, but the random delay on explosions of Rumbler Grenades makes it hard to ensure each hit is attributed to the correct attack.
Similarly to Force Staves, chronology was quite an important thing to have for hits, but has been postponed for a later update, once a proper solution for this has been found. Force Staves will be first, and then Delayed explosions will follow asap after.
